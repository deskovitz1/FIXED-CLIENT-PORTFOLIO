# Zoltux postcards

Web-ready MP4 copies of the original MOVs in `../preview`. Originals are preserved. The two 2160p clips have been resized to 1080p for smoother gallery playback; the other clips retain their original video quality. All MP4s are silent and prepared for fast-start streaming.

The first five files in filename order play muted on loop in the grid's four corners and center. Still images fill remaining cells without repeats. All postcards also appear in the fullscreen gallery.

Add H.264 MP4 clips here and refresh to include them. Prefix filenames with `01-`, `02-`, etc. to control ordering.
