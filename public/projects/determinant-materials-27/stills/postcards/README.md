# Determinant Materials postcards

Drop short H.264 MP4 clips here, then refresh the website. WebM and M4V are also supported. MOV files need browser-friendly MP4 copies before they appear.

The first five clips, sorted by filename, play muted on loop in the top-left, top-right, center, bottom-left, and bottom-right cells of the 3×3 grid. Unique stills fill the remaining spaces. Use prefixes such as `01-` through `05-` to control the order.

All clips, including extras beyond the first five, appear throughout the fullscreen gallery. Playback pauses offscreen and respects reduced-motion preferences, just like NEOGENESIS and Second Sight.
